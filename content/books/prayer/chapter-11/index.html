11. Sources

The material

The study was built from a collection of research notes on prayer (October 2026). It holds:

  • a set of questions put to the research assistant in the Logos Bible software, on passages where believers remind God of his promises, on unanswered prayer in the light of those promises, and on examples of prayers that were blocked or not granted, with its answers;
  • notes from the same assistant on the forms of prayer and on how prayer is expressed;
  • several dictionary articles, a chapter of systematic theology, a sermon outline, and word studies and commentary notes, listed below;
  • about 990 verses exported from the STEP Bible software, gathered by searching the English Standard Version for the words of prayer and speech.

Reference works

The articles and notes drew on these works:

  • Walter A. Elwell and Barry J. Beitzel, "Prayer", in Baker Encyclopedia of the Bible (Grand Rapids, MI: Baker Book House, 1988), 1745–50. The article quotes the Westminster Shorter Catechism and the hymn writer Joseph Hart.
  • Randy Hatchett, "Prayer", in Holman Illustrated Bible Dictionary, ed. Chad Brand et al. (Nashville, TN: Holman Bible Publishers, 2003), 1320–22.
  • Layton Talbert, "Prayer in the Life of the Church", in Lexham Survey of Theology, ed. Mark Ward et al. (Bellingham, WA: Lexham Press, 2018).
  • Charles Hodge, Systematic Theology, vol. 3 (Oak Harbor, WA: Logos Research Systems, 1997), 692–709.
  • John M'Clintock and James Strong, "Prayer", in Cyclopædia of Biblical, Theological, and Ecclesiastical Literature (New York: Harper & Brothers, 1894), 8:475–76.
  • James Smith and Robert Lee, Handfuls on Purpose for Christian Workers and Bible Students, Series I–XIII, five-volume edition, vol. 9 (Grand Rapids, MI: William B. Eerdmans, 1971), 187–90.
  • Marvin Richardson Vincent, Word Studies in the New Testament, vol. 2 (New York: Charles Scribner's Sons, 1887), 258.
  • Albert Barnes, Notes on the Old Testament: Psalms, vol. 1 (London: Blackie & Son, 1870–72), 351–52.
  • Wilhelm Gesenius and Samuel Prideaux Tregelles, Gesenius' Hebrew and Chaldee Lexicon to the Old Testament Scriptures (Bellingham, WA: Logos Bible Software, 2003), 568.
  • The Lexham Analytical Lexicon of the Hebrew Bible (Bellingham, WA: Lexham Press, 2017).

The research assistant's answers also cited:

  • Gary Inrig, I & II Kings, ed. Max Anders, Holman Old Testament Commentary (Nashville, TN: Holman, 2003), 7:56.
  • Kenneth O. Gangel and Stephen J. Bramer, Genesis, ed. Max Anders, Holman Old Testament Commentary (B&H Publishing Group, 2002), 277.
  • The Spurgeon Study Bible: Notes (Nashville, TN: Holman Bible Publishers, 2017), 1168.
  • C. H. Spurgeon, Morning and Evening: Daily Readings (London: Passmore & Alabaster, 1896).
  • Dennis L. Okholm, "Prayer", in Evangelical Dictionary of Biblical Theology, Baker Reference Library (Grand Rapids: Baker Book House, 1996), 625–26.

Scripture

Quotations are from the English Standard Version, and each has been checked against the ESV text. Most are among the verses gathered for the study or are passages the reference works point to; a few further passages on the same themes have been added, such as those on the posture of prayer in Chapter 4. Where the ESV prints LORD for the divine name, it is written here as "the Lord". The older reference works quote Scripture in the King James Version; their own words are quoted as they wrote them, but the Scripture they cite is given in the ESV.
