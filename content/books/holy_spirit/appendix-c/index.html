C.1 Linguistic Foundation

Hebrew: רוּחַ (ruach) — wind, breath, spirit. Semantic range: physical breath/wind; life force/vitality; disposition/character; immaterial human person; Divine Spirit.

Greek: πνεῦμα (pneuma) — wind, breath, spirit. Semantic range: wind/breath; human spirit/inner person; Holy Spirit; evil/unclean spirits; spiritual beings.

C.3 Category 1: Spirit of God / Holy Spirit (422 verses)

References to God’s own Spirit — the third person of the Trinity. Characteristics: creative power (Gen 1:2, Job 33:4, Psa 104:30); empowerment for service (Judg 3:10, 6:34, 1 Sam 16:13); prophetic inspiration (Num 11:25, 2 Sam 23:2, Acts 2:4); teaching and guidance (Neh 9:20, John 14:26, 16:13); sanctification (Ezek 36:27, Rom 8:13, Gal 5:22); presence of God (Psa 139:7, 1 Cor 3:16); can be grieved (Isa 63:10, Eph 4:30) and resisted (Acts 7:51); personal attributes: speaks, testifies, intercedes (Acts 13:2, Rom 8:26–27, John 15:26).

C.4 Category 2: Evil / Harmful / Unclean Spirits (52 verses)

Malevolent spiritual beings. Sub-types: spirits sent by God as judgement (Judg 9:23, 1 Sam 16:14–16, 1 Kgs 22:21–23); unclean/demonic spirits (Matt 8:16, Mark 1:23–26, Luke 4:33–36); spirits of divination or deception (Acts 16:16, 1 Tim 4:1, 1 John 4:3, Rev 16:13–14); named spirits: jealousy (Num 5:14), whoredom (Hos 4:12, 5:4), deep sleep (Isa 29:10), Antichrist (1 John 4:3).

C.5 Category 3: Human Spirit / Inner Person (175 verses)

The immaterial dimension of human personhood. Sub-categories: (A) Emotional/psychological states (68 verses): troubled, broken, revived, vexed, fainting, jealous. (B) Character/disposition (42 verses): different spirit (Caleb), humble, proud, patient, trustworthy, contrite, willing, hardened. (C) Life force/vitality (25 verses): breath of life, spirit returns to God at death, spirit as lamp of the Lord. (D) Cognitive/volitional functions (18 verses): searches, decides, prays, witnesses, constrains, knows.

C.6 Category 4: Spirits as Beings (9 verses)

Spiritual beings, angels, demons, or disembodied entities: Job 4:15, Luke 24:37, 24:39, Acts 23:8–9, 1 John 4:1–2, 4:6, Jude 19–20.

C.7 Category 5: Special / Theological Passages (15 verses)

Key theological statements: God is spirit (John 4:24), the last Adam a life-giving spirit (1 Cor 15:45), the Lord is the Spirit (2 Cor 3:17). Critical trichotomy passages: 1 Thessalonians 5:23 (spirit, soul, and body explicitly listed) and Hebrews 4:12 (division of soul and spirit).

C.8 Theological Synthesis

The biblical concept of “spirit” encompasses a wide semantic range. The same Hebrew/Greek word covers all uses; context determines meaning. The Holy Spirit is a distinct divine person with personal attributes. Human spirits are individual to each person, surviving death. Evil spirits are individual demonic beings. The relationship between human spirit and soul supports both trichotomist (spirit as God-ward dimension, soul as self-ward dimension) and dichotomist (spirit and soul as overlapping terms) interpretations.
