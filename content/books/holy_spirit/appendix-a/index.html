Appendix A: Complete Spirit Verse Compilation — Old Testament

In this section

  1. A.1 Distribution by Book
  2. A.2 Key Spirit of God References (154 verses)

This appendix provides the complete reference compilation of all Old Testament verses containing “spirit” and related words (ruach and associated Hebrew terms). The raw data was extracted from STEP Bible software using the ESV translation with the search query ~spirit Gen–Mal, yielding 1,358 results across the entire OT canon. Of these, 515 are categorised as direct “spirit” references in the categorisation analysis (Appendix C); the broader dataset includes related words such as breath, wind, life, soul, and heart.

Translation: English Standard Version (ESV)

Hebrew primary term: רוּחַ (ruach) — wind, breath, spirit
