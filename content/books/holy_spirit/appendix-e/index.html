E.1 Primary Biblical Tools

STEP Bible software (stepbible.org). English Standard Version (ESV).

E.2 Historical Sources by Period

Period 1: Ancient Near East

Mari Letters (18th c. BC). Nineveh tablets (7th c. BC). Archaeological evidence.

Period 2: Second Temple

Dead Sea Scrolls: 1QS, 1QH, 1QM, CD, Pesharim. Josephus (Antiquities, Wars). Philo. Rabbinic literature: Tosefta Sotah 13:2.

Period 3: Church Fathers

Justin Martyr (Dialogue with Trypho). Irenaeus (Against Heresies). Tertullian (Against Marcion). Origen. Eusebius (Church History). Chrysostom. Augustine.

Period 4: Medieval

Bernard of Clairvaux. Francis of Assisi. Julian of Norwich (Revelations of Divine Love). Hildegard of Bingen. Catherine of Siena. Waldensian sources. Synod of Toulouse (1229), Council of Tarragona (1234).

Period 5: Reformation

Luther (various; German Bible 1522/1534). Calvin (Institutes). Anabaptist sources. Puritan theology. Tyndale NT (1526). Geneva Bible (1560). KJV (1611).

Period 6: Modern

Edwards (Religious Affections, Faithful Narrative). Moravian records. Finney (Lectures on Revivals). Pew Research Center. Revival Library. Christian History Institute. Asia Harvest, ChinaSource.

E.3 Scholarly Works (Identified for Phase 2)

Pneumatology: McDonnell/Montague, Christian Initiation and Baptism in the Holy Spirit. Dunn, Baptism in the Holy Spirit. Fee, God’s Empowering Presence. Turner, The Holy Spirit and Spiritual Gifts. Welker, God the Spirit.

History: Kydd, Charismatic Gifts in the Early Church. Robeck (Pentecostal history). Keener (Acts commentary).

Revival: Revival Library (revival-library.org). Murray, Revival and Revivalism. Orr (multiple volumes).

E.4 Research Process Sources

Wikipedia (initial orientation). News sources (Asbury 2023, contemporary movements). Systematic web search documented in REVIVALS_COMPLETE_SUMMARY.md.
