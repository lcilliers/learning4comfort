Sources: REVIVALS_COMPLETE_SUMMARY.md, period6 documents, ORIGINAL_CONVERSATION_TRANSCRIPT.txt

D.1 Eighteenth Century

1727 — Herrnhut Revival, Germany. Moravians under Zinzendorf. Continuous prayer meeting (100+ years). Modern Protestant missions launched.

1734–1743 — First Great Awakening. Edwards (Northampton), Whitefield (itinerant). 20,000–50,000 conversions. Founded Princeton, Dartmouth, Brown, Rutgers.

1760s–1770s — Methodist Revival (England/Wales). John and Charles Wesley, Whitefield.

D.2 Nineteenth Century

1800–1840 — Second Great Awakening. Camp meetings (Cane Ridge 1801: 20,000–30,000). Finney (500,000 conversions). Social reform: abolition, temperance, education.

1857–1858 — Great Prayer Meeting Revival. Lanphier, Fulton Street, NYC. One million added to churches.

1859 — Ulster Revival, Ireland.

1875–1890s — D. L. Moody campaigns (Britain, American cities).

D.3 Early Twentieth Century

1901 — Topeka, Kansas. Parham’s Bible school. “Initial evidence” doctrine formulated.

1903–1907 — Korea Revivals (Wonsan, Pyongyang). Foundation of Korean Christianity.

1904–1905 — Welsh Revival. Evan Roberts. 100,000+ conversions. Influenced Korea, Azusa, Russia.

1906–1909 — Azusa Street Revival, Los Angeles. William Seymour. Multi-racial. Birthplace of global Pentecostalism.

1906 — India Revival (Pandita Ramabai). Norway Revival.

D.4 Mid-Twentieth Century

1930s — Balokole Revival, East Africa.

1947–1950s — Healing Revival. Branham, Roberts, Osborn. Latter Rain (1948).

1960s–1970s — Charismatic Renewal enters mainline churches (Bennett, 1960). Catholic Charismatic Renewal (120 million). Jesus Movement (counter-culture youth).

D.5 Late Twentieth Century

1970s–present — Korean Church Explosion. <1% to ~30% Christian. Yoido Full Gospel (800,000 peak).

1970s–present — Chinese House Church Movement. 1 million (1949) to 70–100 million (2020).

1980s — Third Wave / Vineyard (John Wimber).

1994–2000 — Toronto Blessing. Millions visited. Controversial.

D.6 Twenty-First Century

Ongoing — African explosion: 10 million (1900) to 685 million (2020). Nigerian mega-churches 50,000+.

Ongoing — Latin American Pentecostalism. Brazil ~50 million. Argentina, Chile, Guatemala.

Ongoing — Iranian underground church. 500,000–1+ million converts.

Feb 2023 — Asbury Revival, Kentucky. 50,000–70,000 visitors, 16 days. 37+ campuses.

D.7 Global Scale (2020)

644 million Pentecostal/Charismatic adherents — 26% of all Christians. 70%+ in Global South. Christianity transformed from Western to Global South faith.
