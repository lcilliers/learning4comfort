Blood in the Bible

2. Timeline of Blood References in the Bible

This timeline lists significant references to blood throughout the Bible, organized chronologically by book and chapter, highlighting its theological importance.

  • Genesis 4:10: The voice of your brother’s blood cries out from the ground.
  • Genesis 9:4-6: Prohibition against eating blood; life is in the blood.
  • Genesis 15:9-18: Blood in the covenant with Abraham.
  • Exodus 12:7: Blood of the Passover lamb on doorposts.
  • Exodus 24:6-8: Blood sprinkled to seal the Mosaic covenant.
  • Leviticus 1-7: Blood in sacrificial offerings.
  • Leviticus 16: Day of Atonement: blood on the mercy seat.
  • Leviticus 17:11: Life is in the blood; blood makes atonement.
  • Deuteronomy 12:23: Do not eat blood, for the blood is the life.
  • 2 Kings 3:22: Blood seen as a sign in battle.
  • Psalm 51:14: Deliver me from bloodguiltiness.
  • Isaiah 53:12: He poured out His soul unto death (blood imagery).
  • Zechariah 9:11: Blood of the covenant sets prisoners free.
  • Matthew 26:28: This is My blood of the new covenant.
  • Mark 14:24: Blood of the covenant poured out for many.
  • Luke 22:20: Cup as the new covenant in His blood.
  • John 6:53-56: Unless you drink His blood, you have no life.
  • John 19:34: Blood and water flowed from Jesus’ side.
  • Acts 20:28: Church purchased with His blood.
  • Romans 3:25: Christ as a sacrifice of atonement through His blood.
  • Romans 5:9: Justified by His blood.
  • 1 Corinthians 10:16: Cup of blessing as participation in Christ’s blood.
  • Ephesians 1:7: Redemption through His blood.
  • Colossians 1:20: Peace through the blood of His cross.
  • Hebrews 9:12: Entered the Most Holy Place by His own blood.
  • Hebrews 9:22: Without shedding of blood there is no forgiveness.
  • Hebrews 10:19: Confidence to enter by the blood of Jesus.
  • 1 Peter 1:19: Precious blood of Christ as a lamb without blemish.
  • 1 John 1:7: Blood of Jesus cleanses us from all sin.
  • Revelation 1:5: Freed us from sins by His blood.
  • Revelation 7:14: Robes washed in the blood of the Lamb.
  • Revelation 12:11: Overcame by the blood of the Lamb.
  • Revelation 19:13: Garment dipped in blood.
