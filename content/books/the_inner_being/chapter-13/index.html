13. Under God's anger

This chapter is now part of 10.13 "Anger", in its section Living under God's anger. The whole of it moved there on 2026-10-05: how God's anger is provoked, pleading and standing in the breach, reading the ruin, dread and trembling, bearing it, the heart that turns, returning, shelter, the hand that is felt, and deliverance from the wrath to come.

The chapter number is kept for now, so that references to Chapter 13 elsewhere in the book still lead here, and from here to 10.13.
