The material

The study was built from a collection of research notes on the presence of God, kept in Zotero as "logos assistant" (23 October 2026). It holds:

  • a set of questions about the presence of God put to the research assistant in the Logos Bible software, and its answers;
  • entries from reference works on divine presence, glory, God's omnipresence and the Spirit's indwelling;
  • about 2,400 verses gathered by searching the English Standard Version for the words "presence", "face", "before" and "in the sight of".
  • three articles added later, on the presence of God, holiness, and the Charismatic movement.

Reference works

The answers and entries drew on these works:

  • Benjamin M. Austin, "Divine Presence", in Lexham Theological Wordbook, ed. Douglas Mangum et al. (Bellingham, WA: Lexham Press, 2014).
  • Gregory R. Lanier, "Glory", in Lexham Theological Wordbook, ed. Douglas Mangum et al. (Bellingham, WA: Lexham Press, 2014).
  • R. B. Gaffin Jr., "Glory", in New Dictionary of Biblical Theology, ed. T. Desmond Alexander and Brian S. Rosner (Downers Grove, IL: InterVarsity Press, 2000), 507–11.
  • G. L. Bray, "God", in New Dictionary of Biblical Theology, ed. T. Desmond Alexander and Brian S. Rosner (Downers Grove, IL: InterVarsity Press, 2000), 512–13.
  • W. H. Bellinger Jr., "Presence of God", in Holman Illustrated Bible Dictionary, ed. Chad Brand et al. (Nashville, TN: Holman Bible Publishers, 2003), 1326–27.
  • Susanne Calhoun, "God's Omnipresence" and "The Spirit's Indwelling", in Lexham Survey of Theology, ed. Mark Ward et al. (Bellingham, WA: Lexham Press, 2018).
  • Bruce N. Fisk, "Building", in Evangelical Dictionary of Biblical Theology, Baker Reference Library (Grand Rapids: Baker Book House, 1996), 76.
  • Gustav Friedrich Oehler and George E. Day, Theology of the Old Testament (New York; London: Funk & Wagnalls, 1883), 128.
  • Walter A. Elwell and Barry J. Beitzel, "Presence of God, The", in Baker Encyclopedia of the Bible (Grand Rapids, MI: Baker Book House, 1988), 1750–52.
  • K. E. Brower, "Holiness", in New Bible Dictionary, ed. D. R. W. Wood et al. (Leicester; Downers Grove, IL: InterVarsity Press, 1996), 477–78. The article cites G. D. Fee, God's Empowering Presence (1994).
  • T. A. Smail, "Charismatic Movement/Theology", in New Dictionary of Theology: Historical and Systematic, ed. Martin Davie et al. (London; Downers Grove, IL: Inter-Varsity Press, 2016), 161–62. The article draws on J. D. G. Dunn, Baptism in the Holy Spirit (London, 1970).

Scripture

Quotations are from the English Standard Version, taken from the verses gathered for the study, or, for Psalm 24:3–4, as quoted in the article on holiness. Where the ESV prints LORD for the divine name, it is written here as "the Lord". Passages that were not among the gathered verses are referred to and described, but not quoted.
