1. Project Vision & Purpose

This project is a deep research study into forgiveness, explored from multiple angles: behavioural/psychological science, biblical theology (how God expresses His own forgiveness), and biblical anthropology (how human forgiveness is described and expected in Scripture). The biblical research includes in-depth study of the original Hebrew and Greek languages and covers both Old and New Testaments.

The end product is an easy-to-read narrative study guide consisting of sequential chapters that build on each other, culminating in a final practical support chapter. Each chapter will cross-reference supporting research resources, including Bible verse references with original language analysis, and references to scientific and other knowledge sources. An appendix will document the full research process, technology used, the cooperation between researcher and AI, and dates of the study.

2. Research Methodology

2.1 Core Principles

  • Depth over breadth: thorough investigation rather than surface-level sampling
  • No assumptions: every claim must be substantiated or clearly qualified
  • If something is incomplete or inconclusive, it must be explicitly stated as such
  • All statements must be validated and cross-referenced where necessary
  • Multiple viewpoints must be explored, including dissenting perspectives
  • No guessing or hallucination – ask for clarification when uncertain
  • When multiple versions or perspectives exist, highlight sources and assumptions

2.2 AI Limitations & Mitigation (from Holy Spirit Project)

The Holy Spirit research project identified specific AI limitations that are actively mitigated in this project:

  • Incomplete source data: AI tends to find 2–3 supporting examples and stop. Mitigation: researcher prompts for exhaustive coverage; AI flags when it has only sampled rather than completed.
  • Premature synthesis: AI summarises rather than preserving raw data. Mitigation: Phase 1 gathers data without interpretation; raw findings are documented before conclusions.
  • Hypothesis-driven bias: AI wants to “solve” quickly. Mitigation: discovery-driven exploration before forming conclusions; data is allowed to challenge frameworks.
  • Embedded assumptions: When raw data is not preserved, AI assumptions become hidden. Mitigation: transparent documentation of all assumptions; researcher verification at each stage.

2.3 Research Phases

The project follows a strict phased approach. Data gathering and interpretation are kept separate to prevent premature conclusions from shaping data collection.

PhaseActivityDescription
1Research & CollationGather data across all three research pillars. This phase gathers data rather than interprets it. Interactive process with researcher evaluating preliminary results and adding prompts for further research.
2Analysis & EvaluationAnalyse and evaluate the collected research. Identify patterns, tensions, and connections across the three research domains.
3Narrative ChaptersCompile the results into a series of chapters. Each chapter built in a separate chat. Consistent style across chapters.

2.4 Working Model: Hybrid (Option C)

Agreed during the Holy Spirit project: the researcher (Leroux) leads the research and retains authority as principal researcher and author. AI assists with organisation, structure, synthesis, and drafting. The researcher verifies and corrects AI interpretations.

3. Research Scope

3.1 Central Theme

Forgiveness is the central theme. The research explores all related aspects, including but not limited to:

  • Unforgiveness
  • Retribution
  • Reconciliation
  • Atonement
  • Other elements as discovered during research

3.2 Dimensions of Inquiry

  • The nature of forgiveness – what it is
  • What forgiveness is NOT
  • The causes that create the need for forgiveness
  • The effects/consequences of forgiveness
  • The consequences of the lack of forgiveness (unforgiveness)
  • Barriers to forgiving

4. Three Research Pillars

PillarFocusApproach
1. Psychology / Behavioural ScienceHow modern science understands forgiveness – its mechanisms, benefits, barriers, and therapeutic approachesEvidence-based findings from peer-reviewed research; acknowledge where science maps the territory but cannot provide the transformative power
2. God’s Own Forgiveness in the BibleHow God expresses, models, and enacts His own forgiveness throughout ScriptureOld and New Testament study; original Hebrew and Greek language analysis; theological frameworks
3. Human Forgiveness in the BibleHow forgiveness of/by humans is described, commanded, and enabled in ScriptureBiblical anthropology; the role of spirit, soul, and body (trichotomy); the Holy Spirit’s enabling role; the fruit of the Spirit

5. Building on Prior Research

This project builds on:

  • Results already included in this project (from prior conversations, some partially lost due to technical issues)
  • Research done for related projects – the Holy Spirit study and the Spirit/Soul/Body study – material uploaded as needed
  • Recovery of original research that was lost – filling gaps by redoing research where necessary

5.1 Known Prior Work (Recovered)

  • A five-part study guide framework was developed (now adopted as the chapter structure below)
  • The Holy Spirit’s role in “quickening” the human spirit to enable forgiveness was explored
  • Key passages studied: Galatians 5:22-23, Ephesians 4:32, Colossians 3:12-17
  • Connection made to the trichotomy framework (spirit, soul, body) from the separate anthropology study
  • Title proposed: “Forgiveness: A Study Guide for Those Who Struggle – From Human Limitation to Divine Enablement”

5.2 Holy Spirit Research: Key Findings Relevant Here

A separate summary document has been produced (Holy_Spirit_Research_Summary_for_Forgiveness.docx) detailing:

  • Trichotomy validated: soul sins directly, spirit does not – unforgiveness resides in the soul
  • Soul is self-ward, spirit is God-ward – explains why forgiveness feels humanly impossible
  • Epigenetics: 3–4 generation trauma pattern matches Exodus 20:5 – unforgiveness has biological consequences
  • Christ’s work bridges creation anthropology and Spirit’s outpouring – the mechanism for divine forgiveness
  • OT to NT Spirit shift: from temporary to permanent indwelling – different economy for forgiveness
  • Seven categories of Spirit’s work – the enabling dimensions for forgiveness

6. Chapter Structure & Chat Plan

Note: This structure is the working chapter plan derived from prior research. It may be adjusted, expanded, or reorganised after Phase 1 (research) and Phase 2 (analysis) are complete, if the evidence demands it. The research scope (Section 3) is broader than this structure – topics like unforgiveness, retribution, reconciliation, and barriers will be woven into the appropriate chapters or may warrant additional chapters.

Introduction

Chat: To be determined (may be written last, after all chapters are complete)

Sets the scene for the study guide. Introduces the central question: why is forgiveness so difficult, and is there a way through? Establishes the approach – drawing on psychology, Scripture, and the Holy Spirit’s work. Introduces the reader to the journey ahead.

Part 1: Psychology of Forgiveness

Chat: "Part 1 – Psychology of Forgiveness"

Focus: Human understanding and limitations

This chapter establishes what modern behavioural and psychological science has discovered about forgiveness – its mechanisms, measurable benefits, barriers, and therapeutic approaches. It builds the case that while psychology maps the territory of forgiveness with increasing precision, it ultimately encounters a ceiling: the human capacity to forgive has real limits that science acknowledges but cannot overcome.

Key elements:

  • Bring in earlier research findings from the Holy Spirit project (especially epigenetics)
  • Develop the outstanding questions that psychology cannot answer
  • Build the “human impossibility” case – the gap between knowing we should forgive and being able to
  • Nature, causes, effects, and barriers to forgiveness from a scientific perspective
  • What forgiveness is NOT (psychological misconceptions)
  • Consequences of unforgiveness (health, relational, psychological)

Part 2: Biblical Foundation of Forgiveness

Chat: "Part 2 – Biblical Foundation of Forgiveness"

Focus: God’s character and OT/NT progression

This chapter traces the theology of forgiveness through the entire biblical narrative. It examines how God reveals His own forgiving nature and how the concept develops from the Old Testament sacrificial system through to Christ’s atoning work. Original Hebrew and Greek language analysis is integral to this chapter.

Key elements:

  • Deep dive into key OT and NT passages with original language analysis
  • Trace forgiveness theology through Scripture: creation → fall → law → prophets → Christ
  • Connect sacrifice → atonement → Christ’s work
  • How God expresses and models His own forgiveness
  • The OT-to-NT shift in the spiritual economy of forgiveness

Part 3: God’s Expectations for Forgiveness

Chat: "Part 3 – God’s Expectations for Forgiveness"

Focus: Commands and standards

This chapter examines what God expects of humans regarding forgiveness. It confronts the tension between the divine command to forgive and the human inability to do so in our own strength – setting up the need for the Holy Spirit’s enabling work in Part 4.

Key elements:

  • Matthew 18 (the unforgiving servant), Ephesians 4:32, Colossians 3:13
  • The “as God forgave you” pattern – the standard is God’s own forgiveness
  • Why we cannot do this alone – the impossibility of the standard without divine help
  • Retribution, reconciliation, and the boundaries of forgiveness
  • Forgiveness and justice – how these relate in God’s economy

Part 4: Holy Spirit’s Enabling Work

Chat: "Part 4 – Holy Spirit’s Enabling Work"

Focus: Divine provision and mechanism

This chapter presents the solution to the impossibility established in Parts 1 and 3. Drawing heavily on the findings from the Holy Spirit research project, it shows how the Spirit enables believers to forgive in ways that exceed natural human capacity.

Key elements:

  • The Spirit’s role as Helper/Counsellor (Parakletos)
  • Fruit of the Spirit enabling forgiveness (Galatians 5:22-23)
  • Colossians 3:12-17 synthesis – the “putting on” passage
  • The trichotomy connection: Spirit quickens the human spirit, which transforms the soul
  • The mechanism: how divine forgiveness flows through the believer

Part 5: Practical Application

Chat: "Part 5 – Practical Application"

Focus: Walking it out daily

The culminating chapter translates the theological and psychological understanding into practical, actionable steps. This is the “so what?” chapter for the reader who is struggling to forgive.

Key elements:

  • Process steps for working through forgiveness
  • Overcoming specific obstacles and barriers
  • Daily practices and spiritual disciplines that sustain a forgiving posture
  • Transformation – what the journey looks like over time
  • Honest acknowledgement that forgiveness is a process, not an event

7. Final Output Format

7.1 Narrative Style

The study guide will be an easy-to-read narrative – accessible to a general reader but intellectually rigorous. It is not an academic paper, nor a devotional. It is a study guide that takes the reader on a journey through the evidence and arrives at practical application.

7.2 Cross-Referencing

Each chapter will include:

  • Cross-references to Bible verses with original language snippets where relevant
  • References to scientific studies and other knowledge resources
  • Acknowledgement of differing viewpoints or interpretations

8. Working Protocol

8.1 Preservation of Work

Every significant piece of work will be saved as a downloadable Word document (.docx). Chat history is not treated as a reliable archive. The researcher saves all documents locally.

8.2 Interactive Research Process

Research is interactive: Claude produces preliminary results, the researcher evaluates them, and additional prompts are given for further research. This iterative loop continues until the research is complete before moving to analysis.

8.3 Chat Structure

Each major topic or chapter will be worked on in a separate chat within this project, keeping conversations focused and manageable. The chat plan is documented in Section 6.

8.4 Document Versioning

This framework document will be reissued with a new version number when significant changes are made. The researcher should save each version locally. Research output documents will follow a clear naming convention.
