Privacy policy

Overview

Learning4Comfort is a personal, non-commercial site. It is operated from the United Kingdom. It is designed to collect as little personal data as possible.

What this site does not do

  • It does not set cookies of its own.
  • It does not use advertising, analytics, or tracking tools.
  • It has no accounts, comments, forms, or payments.
  • It does not sell or share personal data.

Reading position stored on your device

As you read a book, this site may save the book, page, and approximate scroll position in your browser's local storage so you can continue where you left off on the same device. This information is not sent to this site or used to identify you. You can clear a book's saved position from its contents page, or remove it using your browser's site-data controls. If browser storage is unavailable, the reading and search features remain usable, but a continue-reading link may not be available.

What may be collected

  • Hosting logs. GitHub Pages may record basic access information such as your IP address, the pages visited, and your browser type. This is handled by GitHub under the GitHub Privacy Statement. I do not have access to these logs.
  • Email. If you email me, I receive your address and message. I use them only to reply, and I do not share them. I will delete them on request.

Third-party links

Some pages link to external websites. I am not responsible for their privacy practices.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children, and I do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your rights (UK and EU GDPR)

You may ask what personal data I hold about you (normally only an email you have sent), ask for it to be corrected or deleted, or complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). Email leroux@cilliers.co.uk for any of these.

Changes

If features are added, such as a contact form or analytics, this policy will be updated with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this page, credits, or privacy: leroux@cilliers.co.uk or the Feedback page.
